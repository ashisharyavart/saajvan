Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Saajvan Design Studio ("we," "our," or "us"). Based in Gurugram, Haryana, we provide luxury residential and commercial interior architecture, space planning, and 3D visualization services across Gurgaon and Delhi NCR. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us through our website, consultations, or direct communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when inquiring about our interior design services, scheduling a consultation, or reaching out via email, phone, or WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, phone number, and location/address of your project.
  • Project Details: Floor plans, space dimensions, design preferences, budget range, and timeline expectations shared during initial discussions.
  • Technical Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, browser type, and page usage statistics collected automatically when navigating our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business purposes associated with fulfilling your interior design project, including:

  • Responding to design inquiries and booking initial consultation calls.
  • Preparing architectural floor plans, 3D renderings, and material moodboards.
  • Coordinating on-site execution with verified contractors, vendors, and project leads.
  • Improving our website user experience and client communication protocols.

4. Data Sharing & Privacy Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. We only share project data with trusted partners (such as site contractors or material vendors) when essential to execute your project, and only with your prior consent.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and web analytics tools to ensure optimal page performance, fast video playback, and responsive design rendering across devices. You can control or disable cookie preferences directly within your web browser settings.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Physical site documentation and digital design assets are securely maintained.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections to any inaccuracies, or ask for the deletion of your contact records. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
